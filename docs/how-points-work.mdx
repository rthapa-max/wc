# How Points Work

Welcome! This guide explains how scoring works when you predict match scores. Read it once and you’re good to go.

## Quick summary

| Points | What you did |
| --- | --- |
| **3** | Exact score — your predicted score matches the final result |
| **2** | Correct outcome — right winner or draw, but wrong score |
| **1** | Participated — you submitted a prediction, but the outcome was wrong |
| **0** | No prediction — you did not submit a pick for that match |

Points are only counted **after the match is finished** and the final score has been recorded.

---

## 3 points — Exact score

You get **3 points** when your predicted home and away goals **exactly match** the final result.

**Examples**

- You pick **2–1**, result is **2–1** → **3 points**
- You pick **0–0**, result is **0–0** → **3 points**
- You pick **1–1**, result is **1–1** → **3 points**

Exact score always beats everything else. If you nail the numbers, you get 3 — even if it’s a draw.

---

## 2 points — Correct winner (wrong score)

You get **2 points** when you get the **outcome** right (home win, away win, or draw) but **not** the exact score.

**Examples**

- You pick **3–2**, result is **2–1** → your team wins **2 points**
- You pick **0–0**, result is **1–1** → draw prediction correct → **2 points**
- You pick **1–3**, result is **0–2** → your team wins → **2 points**

---

## 1 point — Participated

You get **1 point** whenever you **submit a prediction** for a finished match, even if both the score and the outcome are wrong.

**Examples**

- You pick **2–0**, result is **1–2** → wrong winner and wrong score → **1 point**
- You pick **0–0**, result is **2–0** → wrong outcome → **1 point**
- You pick **3–1**, result is **0–0** → wrong outcome → **1 point**

**Important:** A **0–0** prediction is a valid pick. Leaving both boxes empty and saving also counts as **0–0**. You still earn at least **1 point** for participating if the final result is different.

---

## 0 points — No prediction

You get **0 points** for a match if you **did not submit a prediction** before the window closed.

No pick = no points for that match.

## How to submit a prediction

1. **Sign in** to the app.
2. Open the **Fixtures** section and find the match.
3. Enter the **home** and **away** score (use **0** for nil — e.g. **0–0** is a draw).
4. Click **Save prediction**.

You can change your pick anytime while predictions are **open** for that match.

### When predictions open and close

- **Open:** 3 days before kickoff
- **Close:** 1 hour before kickoff

After the window closes, picks are locked for that match. Make sure you save before the deadline.

---

## Leaderboard

Your **total points** are the sum of points from all finished matches where you submitted a prediction.

Rankings on the leaderboard are ordered by:

1. **Total points** (highest first)
2. **Exact scores** (3-point picks on finished matches)
3. **Number of predictions** submitted

---

## Tips

- **Draws count.** Predicting **0–0**, **1–1**, or any equal score means you’re picking a draw.
- **Every pick matters.** Even a wrong prediction earns **1 point** — participation adds up over the tournament.
- **Save early.** Don’t wait until the last hour before kickoff.

---

## Questions?

If something looks wrong with your points after a match is marked finished, double-check that:

- You saved your prediction before the window closed
- The admin has entered the **final score** for that match

Good luck — and may the best predictor win!
